Exploring Surabaya and Beyond

Tugu Pahlawan (Heroes Monument)

The main symbol of Surabaya, dedicated to the people who died during the Battle of Surabaya on November 10, 1945. The monument is surrounded by a museum showcasing the history of Indonesia's struggle for independence. It is an essential visit to understand the spirit of the city.

Suramadu National Bridge

Connecting Surabaya on the island of Java to the neighboring island of Madura, this is the longest bridge in Indonesia. Driving across it, especially during sunrise or sunset, offers spectacular views of the Madura Strait. It represents the rapid modernization of the region.

Mount Bromo (Regional Attraction)

While not located inside the city borders, Surabaya is the primary gateway for trips to Mount Bromo. This active volcano is famous for its otherworldly sunrise views and vast sea of volcanic sand. It is a mandatory weekend trip for any student or tourist staying in Surabaya.
