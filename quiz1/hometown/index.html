Surabaya: My Study Town

While originally from France, Surabaya has become my academic home.

Located on the northeastern border of Java island, Surabaya is the second-largest city in Indonesia. It is famously known as "Kota Pahlawan" (City of Heroes) due to the importance of the Battle of Surabaya in galvanizing Indonesian and international support for Indonesian independence.

Today, Surabaya is a bustling metropolis that perfectly blends rich historical heritage with modern infrastructure. It serves as a major educational and technological hub in East Java, making it an incredible environment for an engineering student to live, study, and grow both professionally and culturally.

Quick Facts

  • Location: East Java, Indonesia
  • Status: 2nd Largest Indonesian City
  • Nickname: Kota Pahlawan
  • Vibe: Dynamic, industrial, and historical
