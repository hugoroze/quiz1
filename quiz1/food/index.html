Culinary Delights of Surabaya

Rawon

Rawon is a rich, savory beef soup that is iconic to East Java. Its defining characteristic is its dark black color, which comes from the use of 'keluak' nuts. The nuts give the soup a deep, nutty, and slightly earthy flavor. It is typically served with rice, bean sprouts, salted egg, and spicy sambal.

Rujak Cingur

A unique traditional salad, Rujak Cingur is a mix of sliced fruits, vegetables, tofu, and tempeh, topped with a distinctive ingredient: 'cingur' (boiled beef snout). Everything is smothered in a savory, sweet, and spicy sauce made from fermented shrimp paste (petis), peanuts, and chili.

Sate Klopo

Sate Klopo translates to coconut satay. It features skewers of beef or chicken that are marinated and coated with grated coconut before being grilled over charcoal. The coconut adds a rich, savory, and sweet caramelization to the meat, creating a flavor profile entirely unique to Surabaya.

Lontong Balap

This traditional dish consists of lontong (compressed rice cakes), fried tofu, lentho (fried cassava and bean patties), and a generous portion of bean sprouts. It is drenched in a sweet soy sauce and petis broth, usually accompanied by a side of clam satay.
